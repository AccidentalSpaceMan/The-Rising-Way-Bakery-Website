Our Story

Building A Future,
One Rise at a Time

We started in our home kitchen as a way to feed our family wholesome, precision baked bread and it has grown into something we're so proud to share with all of you. While sourdough is where it all began, our oven is usually full of more than just bread: cinnamon rolls, muffins, cookies, and other homemade treats make their way in too.

Everything we bake is made right here in our Northeast Ohio home, in small batches, using simple ingredients and careful measurements to get that perfect texture and flavor every time.

As a family-run cottage bakery, we believe food should be made with intention and shared with the people around us. Serving Summit & Portage County, Ohio. ♥
